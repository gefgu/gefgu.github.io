Fastkit-Mobility. A Rust-accelerated, DataFrame-agnostic library for human mobility analysis: clean GPS traces, find stops and trips, compute movement measures, route on road and rail networks, and simulate mobility. Works with pandas and Polars through Narwhals, and many measures follow scikit-mobility's names. Benchmarked against scikit-mobility on about 4 million Brightkite check-ins, with 40K downloads on PyPI.

```bash
pip install fastmob
```
